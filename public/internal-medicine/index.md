# Internal Medicine

## Clinical Focus & Department Overview
The Department of Internal Medicine and Diabetology provides comprehensive care for acute and chronic conditions, emphasizing preventive care, patient education, and long-term health management.

## Pathophysiology, Diagnostics & Procedures
Diagnostics and clinical management of diabetes, hypertension, liver disorders, fevers, infectious diseases, and 24/7 emergency medical stabilization collaborating with Critical Care.
